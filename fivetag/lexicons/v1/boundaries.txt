# Section "lead-ins": generic openers left OUT of a span (guideline 6.3).
# Section "stops": words that END a verb-led span, because what follows says with
#   what, for whom or where (guideline 6.8 and example 4.1). The words "to",
#   "with" and "when" are deliberately NOT stops: the guideline keeps them inside
#   the span ("Deploy applications to cloud environments", "Collaborate with
#   product and QA teams", "Apply ... principles when designing applications").
# Section "function-words": never part of a knowledge phrase's modifiers.
